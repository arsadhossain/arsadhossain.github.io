About

A little about me

I've spent 13+ years in tech, starting as a software engineer writing Python, Perl and shell on Linux, and moving step by step into cloud, DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering.

Along the way I've worked across global enterprises, a hyperscale cloud provider, consulting and fast-moving startups — running platforms on AWS, GCP and OCI, owning reliability for identity and security services, and building compliant infrastructure in regulated environments.

Today I'm a founding engineer at an early-stage AI startup, building the platform foundation for agentic AI in cloud and air-gapped environments.

What ties it together: I like turning fragile, manual operations into systems that are automated, observable and calm to be on call for.

Approach

How I like to work

  • Automate the second time. If I've done it twice by hand, it belongs in code.
  • Measure what users feel. SLOs over dashboards full of vanity metrics.
  • Make incidents boring. Clear runbooks, good alerts and blameless reviews.
  • Secure by default. Least privilege and auditability built in, not bolted on.
  • Keep it simple. The best platform is the one teams don't have to think about.
  • Keep learning. Currently going deeper on Go and AI infrastructure.
